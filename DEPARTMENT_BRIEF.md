# Department Brief – CNSRG Faculty-Led Research Vertical

## Initiative:
SARMF – Smart Contract Automated Remediation and Mitigation Framework

## Objective:
To establish a reproducible smart contract security engineering pipeline aligned with international research standards.

## What Has Been Achieved:

1. DOI-indexed methodology (protocol publication)
2. DOI-archived software artifact (Zenodo release)
3. Structured GitHub repository with reproducibility scaffolding
4. Benchmark dataset framework (SWC-aligned vulnerability coverage)
5. Paper draft prepared for Scopus/WoS-targeted submission

## Research Focus (Current Phase):

- Deterministic vulnerability detection
- SWC normalization
- Automated mitigation modeling
- Pre/post security delta benchmarking
- Empirical evaluation for journal publication

## Operating Model:

Currently functioning as a faculty-led research vertical under CNSRG.
Student onboarding will resume after benchmark stabilization.

## Strategic Value to Department:

- Strengthens blockchain and cybersecurity research portfolio
- Adds DOI-backed infrastructure-level research output
- Improves publication quality through empirical benchmarking
- Supports future funding proposals in secure digital systems
- Positions department in reproducible security engineering research
